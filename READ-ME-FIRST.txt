ProcureNXT - Persona-wise Training Material
===========================================

Open index.html to use the library. It works three ways:

1. Straight from this folder. Double-click index.html. No internet needed.
2. On a shared drive (Google Drive, SharePoint, a network share).
   Keep the folder together; the links are relative.
3. On any web host (Vercel, Netlify, GitHub Pages, an internal server).
   Upload the whole folder and point the site at index.html.

Nothing here depends on claude.ai, and no one needs to sign in.

Folder contents
  index.html          master page, lists the 6 personas
  personas/*.html     one page per persona, with Open PDF and Download
  manuals/*.pdf       the 30 manuals
  assets/             logo and heading font

Known issues in the source manuals (not introduced by this build):
  - Every manual page carries a Scribe "FREE PREVIEW" watermark.
    These were exported on a free Scribe plan and need re-exporting
    from a paid account before going to a client.
  - Four manuals point at the QA environment (ril-qa.ag-ri.in) rather
    than production: How to approve an RFX, How to create an RFA request,
    How to approve an RFA request, and How to create and publish an RFI
    and Auction in ProcureNXT.
